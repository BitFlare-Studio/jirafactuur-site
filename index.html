From logged hours to a paid invoice

Set up once, then invoice straight from a work item. Most teams send their first invoice within ten minutes.

Step 01

Open the settings

After installing from the Atlassian Marketplace, a Jira admin opens the app's settings once.

  1. Click the gear icon at the top right of Jira and choose Marketplace apps.
  2. In the sidebar under Apps, choose Jira Factuur.
Step 02

Company and VAT

These details appear on every invoice. The setup wizard walks you through them, or you can fill in the form directly.

  1. Fill in your company name, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number and address.
  2. Under VAT and payment, pick your VAT regime and add your VAT number and IBAN. Standard, reduced, zero, reverse charge, KOR and a custom rate are all supported.
  3. Set your payment term and default hourly rate, then click Save on each card.
Step 03

Emails and reminders

Write the email your client receives with the PDF. Placeholders in curly braces are filled in per invoice.

  1. On the Email tab, set the subject and body. Click a placeholder chip to insert it.
  2. Under Reminders, switch on Send payment reminders automatically. Reminders go out by themselves once an invoice is past its due date.
Optional

Make it yours

The rest of the Settings tab has sensible defaults, so you can skip it and come back any time.

Invoice look

Pick Letterhead, Ledger or Minimal, an accent colour and your logo. Every template also works without a logo. The live preview next to the form updates as you change it, and you can download a test invoice.

Roles

Add roles such as Designer or Developer, each with an hourly rate. When declaring, pick a role instead of typing a rate. Switch off Show amounts if colleagues declare invoices but shouldn't see rates.

Invoice numbering

Choose how numbers are built, for example 2026-022 or 2026-09-022. Numbers never repeat, and a change only applies to new invoices.

Step 04

Mark work billable

The app adds two fields to your work items. Log time as usual; these decide what ends up on the invoice.

  1. Billable is on by default. Switch it off for internal work you don't charge for.
  2. Invoice description replaces the work item's summary on the invoice. Leave it empty to use the summary.
Tip: if the fields don't show, a Jira admin can add them to your project's screens from the app's settings page.
Step 05

Declare an invoice

Open any work item and choose Declare an invoice. For an epic or parent, every billable child item with logged time becomes a line.

  1. Pick the client under Bill to, or click Add a client to create one on the spot.
  2. Check each line. Choose a role to use its rate, or pick Own rate and type one in. You can change hours, description and a discount per line.
  3. Add a discount on the whole invoice if needed, then click Issue invoice.
Before you issue: the app warns you when work was already on an earlier invoice or isn't done yet. You can still issue the invoice; the warning is there so nothing is billed twice by accident.
Step 06

Track and send

The Invoices tab lists everything you've issued, with what's outstanding at the top.

  1. Click Export on a row to download the PDF, email it to the client, or send a reminder now.
  2. Click Mark as paid when the money arrives. Paid invoices stop getting reminders.
  3. Use Export all as CSV for your accountant.
07

Good to know

Discounts

Give a discount per line, on the whole invoice, or both. Discounts are taken off before VAT.

Privacy & security

Your invoices stay in your Jira. Here's how Jira Factuur handles your data, and how it's secured.

01

Privacy

The short version

Jira Factuur runs on Atlassian Forge, inside your Jira Cloud site. Your company details, clients, roles and invoices are stored in Atlassian's storage for your site. We have no database of our own and no way to read it.

The app sends something outside Atlassian in one case only: when you press Send to client, the invoice email and its PDF go out through our email provider.

What the app stores

All of it is stored in Atlassian's storage for your Jira site, and none of it is shared between sites.

  • Your company profile: name, address, KvK and VAT numbers, IBAN, logo and invoice footer.
  • Your clients, and the roles and rates you define.
  • Issued invoices, the invoice number counter and your reminder settings.
  • Two Jira fields the app adds to work items: Billable and Invoice description.

No analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The app has no cookies of its own.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you uninstall the app. Atlassian then deletes the app's storage for your site. PDFs you downloaded and emails you sent are outside the app and stay where they are.

When support sees your data

Only with your permission, and only what you choose to share with us, such as a screenshot or an exported invoice. We delete it once your question is solved.

Error logs

Forge keeps standard logs from the app's functions. We use them only to find and fix errors.

Your rights under the GDPR

You're the controller of the data in your invoices, and we process it on your behalf. To exercise a right or ask a question, email info@bitflare.nl. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

02

Security

Hosted on Atlassian

The app is built on Forge, so it runs on Atlassian's infrastructure, with Atlassian's hosting, encryption and access controls. We have no servers of our own to attack or leak.

As few permissions as possible

The app asks only for the permissions listed above. Anything that changes a work item runs as you, so Jira applies your own access rights.

PDFs are made in your browser

When you download an invoice, the PDF is created on your own computer. It is only sent anywhere when you choose Send to client.

One outside connection

The app can only reach api.resend.com, which is declared in its manifest. Forge blocks every other outside address.

Report a security issue

Found a vulnerability? Email info@bitflare.nl with "Security" in the subject line. We'll confirm we got it within one business day. Please give us time to fix it before you make it public.
