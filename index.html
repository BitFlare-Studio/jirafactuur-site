How it works

Configure your company once. From then on, invoice straight from any work item, epic, sprint or project — built from hours you actually logged, never estimates, never billed twice.

1

Set up your company — once

Open Jira admin settings → Marketplace apps → Jira Factuur. Run the setup wizard or fill in the form yourself: company details, VAT regime, IBAN and a default hourly rate. Add your clients and customise the email and reminder texts here too — you won't need to touch this page again to invoice.

2

Mark what's billable

Every work item gets two fields: Billable (on by default — switch it off for work that shouldn't be charged) and an optional Invoice description to clarify what a line means to the client.

3

Declare an invoice

Trigger Declare an invoice from a work item, epic, sprint or project. Hours come only from logged time, one line per item — so work already on an earlier invoice is flagged, never charged twice. Review rates, discounts and VAT, then issue.

4

Get paid

The project's Invoices tab tracks every invoice's status — open, overdue or paid. From there, email the PDF straight to your client with a payment QR code attached, or download it yourself, and turn on automatic reminders before and on the due date.

Privacy & security

Your invoices stay in your Jira. Here's how Jira Factuur handles your data, and how it's secured.

01

Privacy

The short version

Jira Factuur runs on Atlassian Forge, inside your Jira Cloud site. Your company details, clients, roles and invoices are stored in Atlassian's storage for your site. We have no database of our own and no way to read it.

The app sends something outside Atlassian in one case only: when you press Send to client, the invoice email and its PDF go out through our email provider.

What the app stores

All of it is stored in Atlassian's storage for your Jira site, and none of it is shared between sites.

  • Your company profile: name, address, KvK and VAT numbers, IBAN, logo and invoice footer.
  • Your clients, and the roles and rates you define.
  • Issued invoices, the invoice number counter and your reminder settings.
  • Two Jira fields the app adds to work items: Billable and Invoice description.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you uninstall the app. Atlassian then deletes the app's storage for your site. PDFs you downloaded and emails you sent are outside the app and stay where they are.

When support sees your data

Only with your permission, and only what you choose to share with us, such as a screenshot or an exported invoice. We delete it once your question is solved.

Error logs

Forge keeps standard logs from the app's functions. We use them only to find and fix errors.

Your rights under the GDPR

You're the controller of the data in your invoices, and we process it on your behalf. To exercise a right or ask a question, email info@bitflare.nl. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

02

Security

Hosted on Atlassian

The app is built on Forge, so it runs on Atlassian's infrastructure, with Atlassian's hosting, encryption and access controls. We have no servers of our own to attack or leak.

As few permissions as possible

The app asks only for the permissions listed above. Anything that changes a work item runs as you, so Jira applies your own access rights.

PDFs are made in your browser

When you download an invoice, the PDF is created on your own computer. It is only sent anywhere when you choose Send to client.

One outside connection

The app can only reach api.resend.com, which is declared in its manifest. Forge blocks every other outside address.

Report a security issue

Found a vulnerability? Email info@bitflare.nl with "Security" in the subject line. We'll confirm we got it within one business day. Please give us time to fix it before you make it public.
