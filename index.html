Built by a freelancer

Made by someone who lost too many Friday afternoons to invoicing.

A freelance developer in the Netherlands tracked every project in Jira, for several clients, then copied the worklogs somewhere else to bill them. Reminders went out by hand, and things slipped. So he built Jira Factuur for himself, and published it so you can focus on the work you love and leave the error-prone, repetitive part to a tool.

More details

Built with Dutch compliance in mind, and just as at home with any freelancer or agency, anywhere, who tracks billable hours in Jira and needs invoices that hold up.

  • Rates per role, for agencies and mixed teams: set a rate for each role once (designer, developer, consultant), then pick the role on each invoice line instead of typing a rate.
  • VAT handled correctly: 21%, 9%, 0%, reverse charge, KOR or a custom rate, each with the required legal statement.
  • Three invoice templates (letterhead, ledger, minimal) in your own colour, logo included, previewed live while you set up.
  • A payment QR code on every invoice, so clients pay in one tap.
  • Email and reminders: the PDF goes straight to your client, with automatic reminders before and on the due date.
  • English or Dutch, for the app and for your invoices.
